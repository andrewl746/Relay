## Pivot 1 — 08:00 · team-specific

**What dropped:**
Problem 8 — Resource Sharing. Useful items sit unused while someone nearby needs the same
thing. Applied to a university campus: students need somewhere to offload furniture and
school materials during co-op terms.

**What we considered:**
A general campus marketplace. A donation/giveaway board. A computed handoff-chain engine
that sequences items between people on rotating schedules (explored as "Chain", not pursued
— too little visible in a 2-minute demo, and it discards the marketplace the problem
statement actually describes).

**What we chose:**
A campus marketplace where **the date is the primary key**. Co-op synchronizes an entire
city — thousands leave and thousands arrive in the same two weeks, three times a year — so
rank by time pressure, match by window overlap, and let prices decay toward a floor.
Extended beyond furniture to cover **the room itself**: sublets, furnished or not. The room
and the stuff in it leave on the same day, so they should move together.

**What we deliberately did NOT change, and why:**
We kept the marketplace framing rather than narrowing to a pure matching engine. The
problem statement is about *resource sharing between people who can't find each other* —
a listing surface is the honest shape of that, and it's what demos in two minutes.
